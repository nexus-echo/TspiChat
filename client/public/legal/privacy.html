1. Who we are

TSPI Digital is operated by TSPI Health Co., Ltd. [registered address; Indian entity or representative, if any] ("TSPI", "we", "us"). For the DPDP Act we are the Data Fiduciary, and for the PDPA we are the Data Controller, for personal data processed through TSPI Digital.

TSPI Digital is used by two kinds of users:

  • Patients (self-service users) who enter their own symptoms and lab results and receive an unreviewed AI-generated TSPI health report.
  • Clinicians and reviewers who analyse de-identified cases and draft treatment plans for physician review.

2. Testing phase notice

TSPI Digital is currently in a testing phase. During testing, AI responses are produced by a third-party model provider (DeepSeek) whose servers may be located outside India and Thailand. Please use test or sample data only and do not enter real health information until we announce that the service is open for live use. Test data will be deleted at the end of the testing phase.

4. Do not enter identifiers

TSPI Digital is designed to work with de-identified health data. Please do not type or upload names, dates of birth, medical record numbers, ID numbers, phone numbers, email addresses or home addresses in conversations or reports. The assistant is instructed to ignore such details and our tools reject requests that appear to contain them, but we cannot guarantee every identifier is caught. If you upload a lab report, cover or remove personal details first where you can.

5. How we use your data

  • To sign you in, keep your account secure and apply the right access for your role.
  • To screen for red flags, run TSPI biological analysis, and generate health reports and treatment-plan drafts.
  • To let you return to earlier conversations and reports.
  • For clinicians: to record reviews, edits, approvals and outcomes in an audit log.
  • To respond to your requests, including requests for doctor review sent to TSPI Digital.
  • To keep the service working, fix errors, prevent misuse and meet legal obligations.
  • To improve TSPI Digital using de-identified or aggregated information only.

We do not sell personal data, do not use health data for advertising, and do not allow our AI model providers to train their models on your data where the provider offers that choice.

6. Consent and legal bases

India (DPDP Act)

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent, given when you accept these terms and this notice, and for certain legitimate uses permitted by the Act (for example, responding to a medical emergency or complying with law). You may withdraw consent at any time (see Your rights); withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out, but we will no longer be able to provide the service.

Thailand (PDPA)

Health data is sensitive personal data under Section 26 of the PDPA. We process it only with your explicit consent, or where another Section 26 exception applies. We process account and technical data to perform our contract with you, for our legitimate interests in operating and securing the service, and to comply with law.

Clinicians

Clinicians who enter case data confirm that the patient has consented to AI-assisted analysis of their de-identified data and that they are permitted to share it.

7. AI processing and drafts

  • Clinical conclusions come from the TSPI AI Brain, a rules-based engine operated by TSPI. A large language model (the "assistant") collects your information, calls the engine and explains the results.
  • Reports and plans are AI-generated drafts. A patient's self-service report has not been reviewed or approved by a TSPI doctor. To request doctor review, contact TSPI Digital.
  • The assistant may run general web searches for medical literature. It is instructed never to include case details in a search.
  • The assistant may run code in a secure sandbox to create PDF reports from your report data.
  • We do not make decisions with legal or similarly significant effects about you solely by automated means; the AI output is information, not a decision.

9. International transfers

Some of our providers process data outside India and Thailand, including in the United States and, during testing, the People's Republic of China. We transfer data only to countries not restricted by the Government of India under the DPDP Act, and, for Thailand, only where the destination has adequate protection or appropriate safeguards are in place as required by Sections 28 and 29 of the PDPA, or with your consent after being told the risks. During the testing phase, please use test data only.

10. How long we keep data

  • Conversations and uploads: until you delete them, or until your account is closed. Deleted items are removed from backups within [30] days.
  • Reports, approvals and audit logs: for as long as needed for care continuity and as required by medical-record and other laws, then deleted or anonymised.
  • Account data: while your account is active and for up to [12 months] after closure for security and legal purposes.
  • Testing-phase data: deleted at the end of the testing phase.

Under the DPDP Act we erase personal data once the purpose is served and you are no longer using the service, unless the law requires us to keep it.

11. Security

We use encryption in transit (HTTPS), single sign-on with role-based access, short-lived access tokens, a 60-minute idle sign-out, access controls that let patients see only their own reports, audit logging of clinical actions, and limited staff access. No system is completely secure. If a personal data breach occurs, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected users as the DPDP Act requires, and the Thai Personal Data Protection Committee within 72 hours where the PDPA requires, and affected users where there is a high risk.

12. Your rights

Depending on the law that applies to you, you have the right to:

  • Access a summary of your personal data and how it is processed, and the identities of those we have shared it with.
  • Correct, complete or update inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erase your data, unless we must keep it by law. You can delete conversations yourself in the app.
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it.
  • Data portability and to object to or restrict processing (PDPA).
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become incapacitated (DPDP Act).
  • Complain to us first through our grievance process, and then to the Data Protection Board of India or Thailand's Personal Data Protection Committee.

To use a right, email privacy@tspi.health from the email address on your account. We may need to confirm your identity. We respond within 30 days, and always within the time the law requires.

13. Children

TSPI Digital is for adults aged 18 or over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the service, contact us and we will delete the data.

14. Cookies

We use only strictly necessary cookies and browser storage for signing in, keeping you signed in, security and remembering settings such as theme. We do not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.
