2. The Service

TSPI Digital Twin is a conversational assistant connected to the TSPI AI Brain, a rules-based clinical decision-support engine. Depending on your role, the Service lets you enter de-identified symptoms and lab results, upload lab reports, screen for red flags, receive a TSPI biological analysis and health report, draft and edit treatment plans, download PDF reports and record follow-up outcomes. Clinical conclusions come from the TSPI AI Brain; a large language model explains them. We may change, add or remove features at any time.
