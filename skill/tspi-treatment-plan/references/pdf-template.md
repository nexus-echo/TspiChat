# TSPI treatment-plan PDF template

Implemented server-side in `packages/api/src/tspi/pdf.ts` (GET /api/tspi/reports/:id/pdf); this file
is the reference for that renderer, not instructions to build a PDF in the chat.

Style follows the TSPI brand (tspi-main.vercel.app / tspi_ui):

| Element | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Primary colour | #001FB8 (TSPI blue): header bar, table header fill |
| Accent colour | #FF8442 (TSPI orange): section rules, status badge |
| Text | #0A0A0A body, #4A5868 secondary |
| Font | Helvetica (built into reportlab); bold for headings |
| Page | A4 portrait, 18 mm margins |

Layout, in order:
1. **Header bar** (blue, white text): "TSPI Digital Twin · Treatment Plan" left; case code and report_id right.
2. **Status line**: "AI DRAFT – pending physician approval" (orange) or
   "APPROVED by <approver> on <date/time>" (blue), exactly as returned by the engine. A patient
   sees "UNREVIEWED AI ANALYSIS – Not reviewed by a TSPI doctor" until approval.
3. **Case summary** table: age band, sex, key symptoms, conditions, medications (no identifiers).
4. **Red flags** section: each flag verbatim, or "None reported by the engine".
5. **Biological assessment** table: axis, status or score, evidence, as returned.
6. **Treatment modules** table: module, dose, duration if given, evidence grade, safety notes.
   For a patient, the engine withholds the plan until a doctor approves and releases it
   (`plan_hidden`): the section is titled "Your treatment plan" and says why instead.
7. **Lab and symptom usage** table (Used / Normal (not scored) / Not scored / Not recognised),
   then **Labs not used for scoring**: each lab in the engine's `unmapped_labs`, with why.
8. **Physician edits** (if any): action, target, reason code, rationale.
9. **Footer** on every page: "Generated by TSPI Digital Twin from TSPI AI Brain output. Clinical
   decisions remain the responsibility of the treating physician." plus page X of Y.

Watermark for drafts: on every page, light grey (#B0B0B0 at 25% opacity) diagonal text
"DRAFT – NOT FOR PATIENT USE" (patients: "UNREVIEWED AI ANALYSIS – NOT MEDICAL ADVICE"), 48 pt,
rotated 45°, centred.

Do not include: patient name, date of birth, MRN, contact details, or anything not returned by the
engine or confirmed by the clinician.
